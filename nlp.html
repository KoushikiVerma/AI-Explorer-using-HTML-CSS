Natural Language Processing

What is it:-

NLP is a branch of AI that enables computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language. It powers things like chatbots, voice assistants (like Alexa & Siri), Google Translate, sentiment analysis, and even spam detection in emails. Think of NLP as the bridge between human communication and machine understanding. Instead of just processing data, computers can now read, write, and even hold conversations!

Natural Language Processing Terminologies:-

  • Tokenization-Splitting text into words or sentences (e.g., “Hello world” → [“Hello”, “world”]).
  • Stemming & Lemmatization- Reducing words to their root forms (e.g., "running" → "run").
  • Named Entity Recongnition(NER)- Identifying names, places, dates, and organizations in text (e.g., “Google” → Company, “Paris” → Location).
  • Sentiment Analysis- Detecting emotions in text (e.g., “This movie is amazing!” → Positive).
  • TF-IDF(Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency)- A technique to measure the importance of a word in a document relative to others.
  • Word Embeddings-Converting words into numerical representations for better AI understanding (e.g., Word2Vec, GloVe, BERT).
  • Speech-to-Text & Text-to-Speech (STT/TTS)- Converting spoken language to text and vice versa (used in voice assistants).
  • Machine Translation-Automatically translating text between languages (e.g., Google Translate).
  • Chatbots & Conversational AI-AI systems that understand and respond to human conversations (e.g., ChatGPT, Google Bard, Alexa).
  • Transformer Models (BERT, GPT, T5, etc.)-Advanced AI architectures that process language more accurately and contextually than traditional models.
